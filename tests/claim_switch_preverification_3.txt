CLAIM SWITCH / PRE-VERIFICATION INTENT TEST SET

Purpose: verify that NLU starts on the very first customer turn, preserves the
pending business intent through identity verification and claim selection, and
supports switching to another authorized claim.

Identity: My name is Margaret Chen. My date of birth is 1985-03-15 and the last four digits of my SSN are 4472.
Pre-verification intent: missing documents -> expected intent: documents
Initial claim: CL-2048

Final check: What is the status of this claim? -> expected status on CL-2055

CASE 303 - Ya Wen Li / P13

Assertions for each case:
- The first turn must produce a non-unclear NLU intent before verification.
- The intent must remain in Session.current_intent or Session.original_intent after verification.
- Claim switching must preserve authorization and populate claim_history.
- The selected claim must be the final active case_id.
